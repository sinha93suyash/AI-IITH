# Category Policy Addendum: Toys

# KARTWAY OPERATIONAL POLICY ADDENDUM: TOYS CATEGORY
**Document ID:** POL-ADD-TOYS-8842  
**Effective Date:** 14 October 2024  
**Applies To:** All Tier 1, Tier 2, and Specialist Support Agents  

---

## 1.0 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This document constitutes a binding category-specific addendum to the Kartway General Customer Operations Manual (POL-GEN-0100). Where provisions in this addendum conflict with the General Manual, the provisions of this addendum shall govern exclusively for transactions classified under the Toys category (Node ID: TY-440 through TY-999).

---

## 2.0 RETURN WINDOW
2.1 The standard Kartway 30-day return window is modified for the Toys category.  
2.2 Customers may initiate a return request for eligible Toy items within **45 calendar days** from the verified delivery date.  
2.3 Return shipping labels generated via the Kartway Resolution Portal expire **14 calendar days** from the date of issuance. If the item is not scanned by the carrier within this period, the return authorization is automatically voided.

---

## 3.0 CONDITION REQUIREMENTS AND PACKAGING MANDATES
3.1 **Original Packaging Requirement:** Unlike general merchandise where generic shipping boxes are permissible, original manufacturer packaging (retail box, blister pack, or tin) is **mandatory** for all Toy returns unless the item arrived damaged or defective.  
3.2 **Grading Conditions:**  
* **New / Unopened:** Factory seals, tape, and shrink-wrap must be intact.  
* **Open Box / Complete:** All internal components, figures, accessories, instruction manuals, and sticker sheets must be present. Missing component claims must be verified via photographic evidence submitted by the buyer within **48 hours** of delivery.  
3.3 Items received with damaged, defaced, or missing original retail packaging that do not meet the defective criteria outlined in Section 3.1 shall be rejected for full refund and subject to Section 5.0.

---

## 4.0 WARRANTY PERIOD
4.1 The manufacturer warranty support window handled via Kartway Concierge Support for non-electronic toys is **90 calendar days** from delivery.  
4.2 For electronic toys, robotic toys, and ride-on vehicles containing rechargeable internal battery systems, the specialized Kartway operational warranty period is **180 calendar days**. Support agents must direct claims exceeding this 180-day threshold directly to the third-party manufacturer using the contact roster in Appendix C.

---

## 5.0 RESTOCKING FEES
5.1 A standard restocking fee of **15%** of the item purchase price shall be deducted from the refund issued to the customer under the following conditions:  
* The item is returned as "No Longer Needed" or "Buyer Remorse" and has been opened.  
* The item is missing non-essential promotional inserts or secondary packaging sleeves, while retaining the primary retail box.  
5.2 Restocking fees are strictly waived for items returned due to verified carrier damage, manufacturing defect, or incorrect item fulfillment by the merchant.

---

## 6.0 HAZARDOUS, CHEMICAL, AND PERISHABLE HANDLING RULES
6.1 **Chemical and Compound Restrictions:** Toy items containing chemical compounds, including slime kits, chemistry sets, crystal-growing kits, and modeling clays, cannot be returned if the internal chemical seal, foil barrier, or airtight container has been punctured, opened, or breached, due to federal safety compliance and contamination risks.  
6.2 **Perishable Elements:** Edible components included in toy playsets (e.g., easy-bake mixes, candy-making refills) are strictly non-returnable and non-refundable. Agents must issue a partial appeasement credit for the calculated value of the edible component rather than demanding physical return of the entire bundled playset.  
6.3 **Battery Safety Protocol:** Returned electronic toys containing lithium-ion or nickel-metal hydride batteries must be inspected for physical swelling, leakage, or terminal corrosion. Agents encountering compromised batteries must flag the return ticket with tag `HAZMAT-TOY-QUARANTINE` and route the item to the designated fulfillment center destruction bin rather than returning it to merchant inventory.
