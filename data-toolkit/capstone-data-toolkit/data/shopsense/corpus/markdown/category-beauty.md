# Category Policy Addendum: Beauty

**KARTWAY OPERATIONS MANUAL**
**ADDENDUM 4.12: BEAUTY CATEGORY OPERATIONAL POLICY**
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2025
**Applies To:** All Tier 1, Tier 2, and Specialist Support Agents

---

### 1.0 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This addendum supersedes standard Marketplace General Policies (Section 2) for all items listed under the Kartway Beauty category, including cosmetics, skincare, haircare, fragrances, and beauty tools. Where this addendum is silent, standard Marketplace General Policies apply.

### 2.0 RETURN WINDOW
2.1 The standard 30-day return window is reduced for the Beauty category. 
2.2 Customers must initiate a return request within **14 calendar days** of the confirmed delivery date.
2.3 Return shipping labels generated via the Kartway Resolution Portal expire **7 calendar days** after issuance; failure to drop off the package within this timeframe voids the return authorization.

### 3.0 CONDITION REQUIREMENTS AND ORIGINAL PACKAGING
3.1 **Original Packaging:** Original manufacturer packaging (including outer cartons, protective seals, and caps) is strictly mandatory for all beauty returns. Items received without original packaging shall be rejected by the returns warehouse.
3.2 **Usage Thresholds:** 
*   **Skincare, Haircare, and Liquid Cosmetics:** May be tested or sampled up to a maximum volume threshold of **10%** of total product capacity. Returns exceeding this usage threshold shall be classified as customer-damaged and denied a refund.
*   **Color Cosmetics (Powders, Lipsticks, Pencils) and Fragrances:** Must be strictly factory-sealed and unopened. Any sign of swatching, spraying, or testing results in immediate return rejection.
*   **Beauty Tools (Hair Dryers, Straighteners, LED Masks):** May be unboxed and tested for operational functionality, but must show zero cosmetic blemishes, hair strands, or skin residue.

### 4.0 RESTOCKING FEES
4.1 A mandatory restocking fee of **15%** of the purchase price shall be deducted from the refund total for all opened or tested skincare, haircare, and functional beauty tools returned in compliance with Section 3.2.
4.2 Unopened items returned in factory-sealed condition are exempt from the restocking fee.

### 5.0 HAZARDOUS MATERIALS (HAZMAT) AND PERISHABLE HANDLING
5.1 **Aerosols and Flammables:** Pressurized containers (e.g., hairspray, dry shampoo) and alcohol-based fragrances exceeding **100 milliliters** are classified as restricted Hazmat items and cannot be returned via standard postal drop-offs. Agents must issue a specialized hazardous return label or grant an immediate refund without requiring physical return, subject to fraud verification.
5.2 **Perishable and Organic Goods:** Organic beauty products containing raw botanical extracts without synthetic preservatives carry a strict shelf-life classification. Return requests for organic items submitted after **7 calendar days** from delivery shall be automatically denied due to degradation risk.

### 6.0 WARRANTY PERIOD
6.1 Consumable beauty products (creams, liquids, powders, perfumes) carry no post-delivery warranty once opened.
6.2 Powered beauty tools (devices with electrical components or rechargeable batteries) carry a manufacturer defect warranty period of **180 calendar days** from the date of purchase, handled directly via Kartway Concierge Service after the 14-day return window has closed.
