# Category Policy Addendum: Furniture

**KARTWAY OPERATIONS MANUAL**
**ADDENDUM 4.8: HEAVY GOODS & FURNITURE CATEGORY POLICY**
*Effective Date: 14 January 2025*
*Applies to: Tier-3 Support Agents, Logistics Exceptions Desk, Vendor Quality Assurance*

---

### 1.0 RETURN WINDOW
1.1 Notwithstanding the standard 30-day marketplace return window set forth in Section 2.1 of the General Terms, all items classified under the Furniture category on Kartway shall be subject to a strict **14-day** return window, commencing on the confirmed date of carrier delivery.
1.2 Return authorization requests initiated on or after the 15th calendar day post-delivery shall be automatically rejected by the automated support tooling, barring verified exceptions outlined in Section 5.0.

### 2.0 CATEGORY-SPECIFIC CONDITION REQUIREMENTS
2.1 To be eligible for return under Section 1.0, furniture items must meet the following structural and cosmetic thresholds:
* (a) Items must be fully unassembled if they arrived in flat-pack format, or maintained in their original erected configuration if delivered fully assembled by Kartway White Glove Freight. Partial disassembly of fully assembled units by the customer invalidates return eligibility.
* (b) Upholstered goods (including sofas, armchairs, and mattresses) must be free from any animal hair, human hair, smoke odor, culinary odors, stains, or structural sagging exceeding **1.5 centimeters** of compression indentation upon inspection at the regional depot.
* (c) Wooden and manufactured wood products must not exhibit screw-stripping, water damage, or structural fracture of load-bearing dowels or cam-lock fasteners.

### 3.0 ORIGINAL PACKAGING MANDATE
3.1 The return of furniture items does **not** mandate the retention or utilization of the manufacturer’s original corrugated cardboard boxes or internal styrofoam bracing.
3.2 Customers are permitted to use alternative packaging materials, provided the item is adequately protected against transit damage. However, failure to utilize adequate protective wrapping resulting in transit damage shall cause the return to be reclassified as customer-damaged under Section 6.2.
3.3 Mattresses must be enclosed in a sealed polyethylene mattress bag prior to carrier pickup for sanitary compliance under regional health codes. Unbagged mattresses shall be refused by the freight carrier at the point of collection.

### 4.0 RESTOCKING FEES AND LOGISTICAL CHARGES
4.1 All returned furniture items—excluding those received defective or damaged—shall incur a mandatory restocking and inspection fee equal to **12%** of the gross item purchase price. This fee shall be automatically deducted from the customer’s refund disbursement upon receipt and processing at the central sorting facility.
4.2 In addition to the restocking fee, customers utilizing Kartway Freight Services for return transit shall have a flat logistics recovery fee of **$65.00** subtracted from their refund total to cover heavy-item pickup logistics.

### 5.0 HAZARDOUS, PERISHABLE, AND SPECIALIZED HANDLING RULES
5.1 Furniture items containing integrated electrical components (such as motorized recliners, adjustable bed frames, or LED-illuminated headboards) must have all lithium-ion battery packs and external power supplies disconnected, secured in anti-static bags, and packed within the primary carton prior to carrier collection.
5.2 Items classified under the outdoor teak and hardwood sub-category that have been treated with aftermarket chemical sealants, oils, or preservatives within **72 hours** of the requested return date are classified as hazardous due to off-gassing risks in enclosed freight trailers and shall be strictly refused for return transport.

### 6.0 CATEGORY WARRANTY PERIOD
6.1 Kartway-fulfilled furniture items carry a standard manufacturer defect warranty period of **2 years** from the date of purchase, superseding the 1-year general merchandise warranty.
6.2 This warranty covers structural frame failures, mechanical recliner motor burnout, and joint delamination under normal residential use. It explicitly excludes surface scratching, fabric fading resulting from direct ultraviolet exposure, and commercial-use wear and tear.
