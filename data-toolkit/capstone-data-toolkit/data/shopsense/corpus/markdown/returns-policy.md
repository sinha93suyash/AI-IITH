# Kartway Returns and Refunds Policy

**Kartway Customer Operations Policy Manual**
**Section 2: Returns, Exchanges, and Refunds**
**Version 4.8 – Effective October 1, 2024**

2.1 The standard return window for all merchandise purchased on the Kartway platform is thirty (30) calendar days from the verified date of delivery, unless explicitly modified by clauses 2.2 through 2.5 of this policy.

2.2 Electronics, including computers, tablets, mobile devices, audio equipment, and smart home appliances, are subject to a restricted return window of fifteen (15) calendar days from the verified date of delivery.

2.3 Apparel, footwear, and fashion accessories are granted an extended return window of forty-five (45) calendar days from the verified date of delivery to accommodate fitting and sizing requirements.

2.4 Groceries, perishable food items, opened personal care products, undergarments, and custom-manufactured goods are designated as non-returnable categories. Support agents are strictly prohibited from issuing return authorizations or refunds for items within these categories, except in cases of proven transit damage or food safety contamination reported within twenty-four (24) hours of delivery.

2.5 Third-party marketplace vendors operating on Kartway may enforce return windows distinct from Kartway standard policies, provided their individual storefront policy is equal to or more lenient than the thresholds defined in clauses 2.1 through 2.3. Agent overrides for third-party vendor windows require prior approval from Vendor Operations Tier 2.

2.6 To be eligible for any return, items must satisfy the following condition requirements:
a) The item must include all original manufacturer packaging, instruction manuals, warranty cards, and accessories.
b) Security seals, barcode stickers, and serial number tags must be intact and untampered.
c) Items must show no signs of physical abuse, liquid exposure, unauthorized modification, or excessive wear outside of normal inspection handling.

2.7 Return shipping fees shall be assessed based on the root cause of the return, allocated as follows:
a) If the return is due to Kartway or merchant error (including wrong item shipped, defective merchandise, or transit damage), Kartway shall cover 100% of the return shipping costs via a prepaid return shipping label generated through the Kartway Resolution Portal.
b) If the return is due to customer remorse, incorrect size ordered, or change of mind, the customer shall bear the return shipping cost. A flat-rate fee of $5.99 shall be deducted from the final refund amount when the customer utilizes the Kartway prepaid return label. Alternatively, the customer may ship the item independently at their own expense, provided a valid tracking number is supplied to Support within seven (7) calendar days of return authorization generation.

2.8 Refund processing times are strictly dictated by the original payment method utilized at the time of purchase, measured from the moment the returned item passes physical inspection at the Kartway Fulfillment Center:
a) Credit and debit cards: 5 to 7 business days for funds to reflect on the cardholder statement.
b) Kartway Digital Wallet: Within 24 hours of item intake confirmation.
c) Cash on Delivery (COD): Exactly 10 business days, contingent upon the customer submitting valid bank account routing and account numbers through the secure Kartway payout portal. Cash refunds are never issued by drivers or at physical drop-off depots.

2.9 Opened, non-defective items that satisfy the conditions in clause 2.6 are subject to a mandatory restocking fee and partial-refund schedule based on product category:
a) Electronics and Major Appliances (opened/unsealed): 15% restocking fee applied to the item purchase price; 85% refund issued.
b) Apparel and Home Goods (tags removed, unwashed, indoor try-on only): 5% restocking fee applied; 95% refund issued.
c) General Merchandise and Toys (factory seal broken, complete components): 10% restocking fee applied; 90% refund issued.

2.10 Items returned missing essential components, manuals, or original packaging accessories that do not render the device inoperable shall incur an additional missing-part penalty fee of 20% of the item purchase price, compounding with any applicable restocking fees defined in clause 2.9, up to a maximum total deduction of 50%. Support agents lack the discretion to waive missing-part penalties without documented authorization from Quality Assurance Supervisor Marcus Vance.
