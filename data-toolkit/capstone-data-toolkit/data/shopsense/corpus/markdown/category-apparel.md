# Category Policy Addendum: Apparel

**KARTWAY INTERNAL OPERATIONS POLICY**
**Document ID:** KW-POL-ADD-042
**Category Addendum:** Apparel
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2025
**Applies To:** All Tier 1, Tier 2, and Escalated Support Agents

---

### 1.0 Purpose and Scope
1.1 This document constitutes the binding category-specific policy addendum for all Apparel items sold on the Kartway marketplace. 
1.2 Where this addendum conflicts with general marketplace guidelines (KW-POL-GEN-001), the rules contained herein shall take precedence for transactions classified under the Apparel department.

### 2.0 Return Window
2.1 The standard return window for all Apparel items is exactly **45 days** from the verified delivery date recorded by the final-mile carrier.
2.2 Return requests initiated on day 46 or later shall be automatically rejected by the Support Portal unless the transaction falls under an approved fraud investigation managed by the Risk Operations team.

### 3.0 Category-Specific Condition Requirements
3.1 Returned Apparel items must satisfy all of the following condition parameters to be eligible for a refund:
    a) All manufacturer tags, brand hangtags, and retail security loops must remain fully attached by their original fasteners. Items returned with detached tags shall be classified as "Used/Non-Resellable" and subject to Section 5.0.
    b) Garments must be free of all foreign odors, including but not limited to cigarette smoke, body odor, culinary aromas, and chemical detergents.
    c) Items must exhibit zero cosmetic blemishes, such as makeup transfer, deodorant residue, pet hair, or thread snags.
3.2 Intimate apparel (including underwear, swimwear bottoms, and hosiery) must retain its hygienic adhesive liner intact. Items missing this liner are non-returnable under health and safety guidelines.

### 4.0 Packaging Requirements
4.1 Original manufacturer packaging (such as branded polybags, presentation boxes, or hanger sleeves) is **mandatory** for all luxury and designer apparel items valued above $150.00 USD.
4.2 For standard apparel items valued at or below $150.00 USD, original packaging is encouraged but not strictly mandatory, provided the item is returned in a secure, opaque mailing bag to prevent transit damage.

### 5.0 Restocking Fees
5.1 A standard restocking fee of **12%** shall be deducted from the customer's refund total if an Apparel item is returned missing its mandatory original packaging (as defined in Section 4.1), or if the item exhibits minor pet hair contamination that requires internal lint-rolling.
5.2 Returns of bulk apparel orders (defined as **6 or more identical units** of the same SKU purchased in a single transaction) shall incur a mandatory **20%** restocking fee regardless of condition, to mitigate wholesale-style inventory cycling.

### 6.0 Hazardous and Perishable Handling Rules
6.1 Unworn apparel treated with specialized waterproofing or fire-retardant chemical coatings must be shipped back in sealed secondary containment bags in accordance with hazardous material return protocol KW-HAZ-09.
6.2 Perishable apparel components (such as real leather goods treated with organic conditioning oils) must be returned within **14 days** of the initial return authorization issuance to prevent structural degradation.

### 7.0 Warranty Period
7.1 The standard manufacturer defect warranty period for all Apparel items sold on Kartway is **90 days** from the date of purchase.
7.2 The warranty covers structural manufacturing failures, including compromised stitching, zipper track failures, and button detachment under normal wear conditions. It expressly excludes accidental tearing, fading caused by improper washing machine temperatures, and normal fabric pilling.
