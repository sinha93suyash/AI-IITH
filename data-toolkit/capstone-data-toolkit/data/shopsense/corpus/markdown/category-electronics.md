# Category Policy Addendum: Electronics

# KARTWAY OPERATIONS POLICY MANUAL
**SECTION 4: CATEGORY-SPECIFIC ADDENDUM – ELECTRONICS**
**Document ID:** KW-POL-04-ELC
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2025
**Applies To:** All Tier 1, Tier 2, and Specialist Support Agents

---

### 4.1 Return Window and Statutory Alignment
4.1.1 The standard return window for all items classified under the Electronics category is strictly **15 calendar days** from the verified delivery date recorded by the carrier. This duration aligns with the general Kartway returns policy.
4.1.2 Return requests initiated on day 16 post-delivery or later shall be automatically rejected by the Support Portal unless the item is covered under Section 4.6 (Manufacturer Warranty).
4.1.3 Return shipping labels generated via the Kartway portal remain valid for **7 calendar days** from the date of issue. If the item is not handed to the carrier within this window, the return authorization is voided.

### 4.2 Category-Specific Condition Requirements
4.2.1 Returned electronic items must meet the "Like-New Functional" standard to qualify for a full refund. This requires:
*   Zero physical damage, scratches, dents, or liquid indicators triggered (LSI status: un-tripped white/silver).
*   All pre-installed operating systems restored to factory default settings.
*   Deactivation of all user security locks, including but not limited to *Find My Device*, iCloud lock, Google FRP (Factory Reset Protection), and biometric profiles.
4.2.2 Units returned with active security locks cannot be processed. Agents must instruct the buyer to remote-wipe the device within **48 hours**. If the lock is not removed, the item is returned to the buyer at their expense, and no refund is issued.

### 4.3 Original Packaging Mandate
4.3.1 Original manufacturer packaging (inner box, exterior retail carton, and custom molded pulp/foam inserts) is **mandatory** for all Television, Audio Component, and Computing sub-categories where the retail value exceeds **$150.00 USD**.
4.3.2 For items under **$150.00 USD**, missing original packaging is permitted subject to a packaging deduction fee, provided all accessories listed in Section 4.4 are present.

### 4.4 Restocking Fees and Accessory Deductions
4.4.1 A standard restocking fee of **15%** of the purchase price shall be applied to all opened, non-defective electronics returns processed under "Buyer Remorse" or "No Longer Needed" reasons.
4.4.2 If a return is accepted with missing standard accessories (e.g., remote controls, power bricks, proprietary charging cables, or detachable stands), the following flat-rate deductions apply and shall be withheld from the refund total:
*   Missing remote control or power supply: **$25.00 USD** per component.
*   Missing proprietary interface cables (HDMI, USB-C, DisplayPort): **$10.00 USD** per cable.
4.4.3 Total deductions under Section 4.4 shall not exceed **50%** of the item's verified purchase price.

### 4.5 Hazardous Materials and Perishable Handling Rules
4.5.1 Lithium-ion, lithium-metal, and lead-acid batteries contained within or shipped alongside electronic equipment are classified as regulated hazardous materials (Class 9).
4.5.2 Returns involving swollen, punctured, or leaking batteries must not be shipped via standard ground or air networks. Agents must issue a specialized Hazmat Return Kit to the buyer via surface transport only.
4.5.3 Defective battery items must be processed by the specialized Hazardous Returns Unit managed by Supervisor Marcus Vance (Extension 4402). Standard frontline agents are prohibited from issuing instant refunds on hazardous-flagged items prior to warehouse inspection.

### 4.6 Warranty Period and Defect Escalations
4.6.1 Kartway provides a direct operational warranty of **90 calendar days** from the date of delivery for all Certified Refurbished and Open-Box electronics sold directly by marketplace merchants.
4.6.2 Brand-new electronics carry no Kartway-backed operational warranty beyond the initial 15-day return window; buyers must be directed to the original equipment manufacturer (OEM) for warranty claims after day 15.
4.6.3 For items failing within the 90-day Kartway warranty window, the merchant is contractually obligated to provide repair, replacement, or full refund within **10 business days** of receiving the returned defective unit at their designated fulfillment center.
